# Survivors

A first-person survival horror game that runs in the browser. You went to gather firewood and came back to an empty camp. Survive 10 days until the helicopter arrives.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) with a keyboard and mouse. It needs an internet connection the first time to load the 3D engine (three.js).

## Controls

- WASD to move, Shift to sprint, mouse to look
- Click or Space to swing your axe, B to block
- 1 weapon, 2 flashlight (click to toggle)
- E interact, F campfire, J fish, G eat, T drink, H medkit
